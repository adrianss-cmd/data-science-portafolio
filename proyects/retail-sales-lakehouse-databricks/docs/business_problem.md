# Business Problem

## Background

An e-commerce company receives transactional data from multiple operational systems, including orders, customers, products, sellers, and payments.

Because the data originates from different sources, inconsistencies and quality issues make it difficult to generate reliable reports for decision-making.

## Objective

Design and implement a Lakehouse pipeline using Databricks that ingests raw data, applies data quality transformations, and produces trusted analytical datasets for business intelligence.

## Expected Outcomes

- Centralized and organized data
- Improved data quality
- Business-ready analytical tables
- Scalable architecture based on the Medallion model
